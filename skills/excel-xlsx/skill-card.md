## Description:

Create, inspect, and edit Microsoft Excel workbooks and XLSX files with reliable formulas, dates, types, formatting, recalculation, and template preservation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and spreadsheet-heavy teams use this skill when an agent must create, inspect, or safely edit Excel, XLSX, XLSM, XLS, CSV, or TSV files where formulas, dates, types, formatting, workbook structure, recalculation, or template preservation matter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Formula or formatting edits in important financial or business spreadsheets can affect downstream results.

Mitigation: Review workbook changes before relying on them, recalculate when current values are needed, and check representative formulas, ranges, and visible formatting.
